## 📌 Overview

This project presents a comprehensive sales performance analysis built using Microsoft Power BI.
The dashboard transforms raw retail sales transactions into interactive visual reports that support business monitoring, operational analysis, and strategic decision-making.

This project demonstrates practical business intelligence and data visualization skills using Power BI tools commonly applied in real-world analytics environments.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Monitor key business KPIs interactively
- Identify top-performing branches and product lines
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Evaluate return rates and operational efficiency
- Explore sales representative performance
- Compare yearly sales trends and profitability
- Build a fully interactive business dashboard for decision-making

## 📂 Dataset Information
The dataset contains transactional retail sales data collected from multiple branches across four different years.

Each year in the dataset represents sales transactions recorded for a single day rather than a continuous yearly dataset, covering multiple branches, product lines, customer types, sales transactions, returns, ratings, and profitability metrics.

Dataset Source: [Sales Dataset](https://github.com/amiraesam/Sales-Analysis-Project/blob/main/Sales%20Raw%20Data.xlsx)

## 📈 Key Business Questions & KPIs
### Sales Performance
- How did sales and profit perform over the years?
- What are the overall sales, net sales, and profit values?
- Which branches generate the highest sales?
- How stable are profit margins across branches?
### Customer Analysis
- Which customer type contributes more to revenue?
- How do sales vary by gender?
- What are the preferred payment methods?
### Returns & Product Analysis
- What is the overall return rate?
- Which product lines experience the highest returns?
- How do product sales vary across cities?
- Which product categories receive the highest customer ratings?
### Operational Performance
- Which sales representatives perform best?
- What are the average representative ratings?
- What are the peak sales hours?
- How does operational performance vary over time?
  
## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Sales and profit showed continuous growth over the analyzed years.
- Operational margins remained relatively stable despite increasing sales.
- Return rates increased alongside business growth.
- Member customers contributed slightly more to total revenue.
- Female customers generated slightly higher sales contribution.
- Cash and E-wallet payments were the most commonly used payment methods.
- Branch performance remained relatively balanced across locations.
- Product performance and customer preferences varied across cities and categories.
- Peak sales activity changed noticeably across different years.
- Food & Beverages required additional operational attention due to weaker sales and higher returns.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed
### Data Preparation
- Cleaned and transformed raw sales data using Power Query
- Created calculated measures and KPIs using DAX
### Dashboard Development
- Built interactive dashboards with dynamic filtering
- Designed KPI cards and business summary visuals
- Implemented slicers for branch, year, and product filtering
- Created interactive charts for trend analysis and comparison
### Business Intelligence Analysis
- Performed sales, profit, and return trend analysis
- Compared branch and product line performance
- Evaluated customer behavior and payment preferences
- Conducted Year-over-Year (YoY) performance analysis
- Generated business insights to support strategic decisions

  ## 📌 Final Conclusion & Recommendations

- The Power BI dashboard revealed strong business growth trends across sales and profitability over the four-year period. Sales and profit increased consistently
  year-over-year while operational margins remained relatively stable.

- The analysis also highlighted a growing return rate alongside increasing sales volume, suggesting the need for stronger return management strategies and
  product quality monitoring.

- Branch performance analysis showed balanced profitability across branches, while customer analysis demonstrated nearly equal revenue contribution between
  member and normal customers.

- Product-level insights revealed that Food & Beverages requires additional operational attention due to weaker sales performance and higher return behavior
  despite receiving strong customer ratings.

The dashboard successfully demonstrates how Power BI can transform raw transactional data into interactive business intelligence solutions that support operational monitoring, performance evaluation, and data-driven decision-making.
